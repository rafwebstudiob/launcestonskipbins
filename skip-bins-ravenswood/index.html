Skip bins in Ravenswood

Ravenswood is about 4 km from central Launceston by road. Here's what to know before you hire a skip, and how to get a free quote.

Last checked 24 October 2026

Skip hire in Ravenswood

Ravenswood is a north-eastern suburb with many older brick and weatherboard homes. House clean-outs, rental turnovers and yard clean-ups are common. Older homes may include fibro, so get it checked before it goes in a bin.

Nearest tip

The City of Launceston's Launceston Waste Centre is on Cavalry Road, Mowbray. Council lists it as open 7:30am to 4:15pm daily, closed Good Friday and Christmas Day, and it has the Uptipity resale shop for reusable goods. Asbestos is accepted only on weekdays between 10am and 2pm, you must register first on 03 6323 3000, and it must be double-wrapped in plastic at least 200 microns thick. Check the City of Launceston website for fees and accepted items.

Council rules

A skip wholly on your own land generally doesn't need approval. On the road, footpath or nature strip, the City of Launceston requires an Occupation of Roads, Parks and Public Reserves Permit, applied for at least 10 working days ahead. See the permit guide.
